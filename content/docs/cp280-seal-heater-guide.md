# CP-280 Case Packer — Seal Heater Troubleshooting

*Demo document — fictional equipment for the TwinForge AI demonstration.*

A slow upward drift in seal-heater temperature (0.1–0.2 °C/h) usually indicates controller thermocouple drift. Recalibrate the thermocouple at the next planned stop. Alarm at 82 °C, trip at 92 °C.

Energy note: the CP-280 draws 6.5 kW at idle. Enable the standby mode after 5 minutes without infeed to reduce idle draw.
