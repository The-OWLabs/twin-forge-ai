# Service Bulletin SB-AX6-114 — Joint-4 Gearbox Bearing Wear

*Demo document — fictional bulletin for the TwinForge AI demonstration.*

**Symptoms:** progressive rise in joint-4 vibration (typically +0.15 to +0.35 mm/s per operating hour once wear begins), rising joint temperature and a 10–20% increase in drive power at constant throughput. A sudden step increase in vibration indicates raceway spalling.

**Risk:** if operation continues past the trip threshold, secondary gear damage is likely and corrective repair typically takes **14–18 h** with expedited parts.

**Recommendation:** schedule bearing replacement within one shift of the alarm threshold being crossed. Reduce speed if the window cannot be arranged immediately.
