# AX-6 Six-Axis Assembly Cell — Maintenance Manual (excerpt)

*Demo document — fictional equipment for the TwinForge AI demonstration.*

## Preventive maintenance interval
- Standard interval: **2,000 operating hours**.
- Estimated duration: **6 h** with a three-person crew (mechanical, electrical, controls).

## Vibration severity guidance (joint drive train)
| Zone | RMS velocity | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | < 4.5 mm/s | Routine monitoring |
| Watch | 4.5 – 7.1 mm/s | Increase monitoring frequency, plan inspection |
| Alarm | 7.1 – 11.2 mm/s | Schedule maintenance; consider derating |
| Trip | ≥ 11.2 mm/s | Protective stop — do not restart without inspection |

## Joint-4 gearbox bearing replacement
1. Lock out / tag out the cell and the upstream conveyor.
2. Remove the joint-4 cover and drain gearbox lubricant; inspect for metallic debris.
3. Replace the output bearing set (kit AX6-J4-BRG) and seal.
4. Refill with specified lubricant, re-torque fasteners, verify backlash.
5. Run the 30-minute break-in program at 60% speed and confirm vibration < 3.0 mm/s.

## Derating
Wear rate in the joint drive train scales approximately with the square of speed. Running at 80% speed reduces the degradation rate by roughly one third while maintenance is arranged.
